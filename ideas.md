# Neat Laundry Design Brief

## Design movement
**Quiet premium utility**: the visual language combines the calm cleanliness of a boutique hotel with the clarity of a modern logistics service. The page should feel dependable and elevated without looking sterile.

## Core principles
- Lead with trust and convenience, not decoration.
- Give every section generous breathing room and a clear next action.
- Use blue as a confident signal color and navy for authority; keep surfaces white and light gray.
- Keep controls obvious, labels explicit, and interactions calm.

## Color philosophy
- Clean Blue `#0284c7` for primary buttons, links, accents, and active states.
- Crisp White `#ffffff` for cards and content surfaces.
- Soft Light Gray `#f8fafc` for section contrast and quiet background fields.
- Dark Navy `#0f172a` for headlines, body contrast, and footer grounding.
- Use pale blue tints for chips, icon containers, and selected states.

## Layout paradigm
A single-column narrative page with a compact max-width container, a sticky translucent header, split hero, bento-style benefits, tabbed pricing, centered booking conversion panel, and a deep navy footer. Use asymmetric image crops and a small floating trust badge for visual rhythm.

## Signature elements
- Pill-shaped blue service labels and buttons.
- A thin blue underline on the active navigation/service state.
- Small numbered process cards and soft blue icon circles.
- A Google-style rating pill with gold stars.
- A framed “fabric care promise” stat card layered on the hero image.

## Interaction philosophy
Immediate, forgiving, and low-friction: CTA links scroll to booking, service tabs update prices without reload, nav collapses on mobile, and booking errors are shown inline before WhatsApp handoff.

## Animation
Use subtle 180–260ms ease transitions, gentle image scale on hover, and a one-time upward fade-in for section reveals. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling non-essential motion.

## Typography system
Use `Inter` via Google Fonts with system fallbacks. Headlines are tight and confident; body text is relaxed with 1.65 line-height. Labels are uppercase with tracking for the premium-service feel. Use `clamp()` for responsive display type.

## Brand essence and voice
Neat Laundry is careful, on-time, and quietly expert. Copy is warm, direct, and reassuring: “We handle the details” rather than loud promotional language.

## Wordmark/logo
Use the supplied Neat logo in the header/footer and as the favicon source where possible. Preserve the original identity rather than redrawing or replacing it.

## Signature brand color
Clean Blue `#0284c7`, supported by a dark navy anchor and abundant white space.
